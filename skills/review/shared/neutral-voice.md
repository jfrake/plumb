# Neutral voice

- Address the authors as "you". Use "I" for the reviewer's view.
- No verdict language, no summary-assessment section, no reassurance paragraph, no closing sign-off.
- No scaffolding between comments ("Turning now to...").
- Do not predict the result of a check you request.
- Spell out niche acronyms on first use.
- Cite to establish, not to prove: one piece of evidence per point.
- Close each comment when the point is made.
- Use no metaphors. Say literally what you mean: name the variable, test, or assumption and the relation (uses, requires, depends on, is measured by). Not "the dictionary that carries H2" but "the dictionary used to measure H2's moderator".
