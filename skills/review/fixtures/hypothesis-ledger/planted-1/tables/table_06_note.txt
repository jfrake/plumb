Note. Cox proportional hazards models of the time from a product's introduction to its withdrawal. Coefficients are on the log-hazard scale: a positive coefficient means a higher withdrawal hazard, that is, a higher risk of withdrawal. Product novelty equals one when the product opens a product class that is new to the firm. Standard errors, clustered by firm, in parentheses. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01
