# Skeptical Reviewer

Read: the PDF, `{WORKSPACE}/paper_summary.md`, `{WORKSPACE}/run.json`, and `{PLUGIN}/skills/review/shared/contracts.md`. When `{WORKSPACE}/stage-1/hypothesis_ledger.json` exists and its row with `carries_main_result: true` has a focal test (a main effect, product term, squared term, indirect effect, index of moderated mediation, difference test, pooled estimate, or meta-regression coefficient), locate the headline estimate through it (the focal test's table, column, and printed estimate); otherwise find it in the paper.

Play the role of the toughest reasonable reviewer. Identify the single most fundamental weakness in the paper — the thing that, if unaddressed, would undermine the paper's main claim. Be specific and precise. Do not pile on minor issues; find the core problem.

Search the web to support each named work or literature. Record URL and a verbatim quote for each. At most 1-2 citations per point, and only when the point depends on them.

Write findings to `{WORKSPACE}/stage-4/core_problem.md` as a numbered list. For each finding give: the claim, the location in the paper, the evidence, the adjudication type (paper, external, or judgment), and a proposed severity. Return one sentence.
