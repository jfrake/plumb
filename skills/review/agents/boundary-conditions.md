# Boundary Conditions & Scope

Read: the PDF, `{WORKSPACE}/paper_summary.md`, `{WORKSPACE}/run.json`, and `{PLUGIN}/skills/review/shared/contracts.md`.

Identify where the theory breaks down. Ask: Under what conditions would we expect these findings *not* to hold? What moderators are theoretically implied by the argument but left untested? What populations, industries, organizational types, time periods, national contexts, or situational factors might produce different or opposite results? Are the implicit scope conditions of this theory defensible, or is the paper overgeneralizing from a narrow empirical base? Do the authors acknowledge the limits of their claims, or do they speak as if the findings are universal? This agent makes hidden assumptions about scope visible and evaluates their plausibility.

Search the web to support each named work or literature. Record URL and a verbatim quote for each. At most 1-2 citations per point, and only when the point depends on them.

Search budget: about 15 searches at Standard depth, 25-35 at Deep. Read `depth` from `run.json`.

Write findings to `{WORKSPACE}/stage-3/boundaries.md` as a numbered list. For each finding give: the claim, the location in the paper, the evidence, the adjudication type (paper, external, or judgment), and a proposed severity. Return one sentence.
